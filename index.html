Meet Erika Wu

NYU Stern
3× International Winner
#1 Test & Roleplay of 200+

Erika Wu is a 3x DECA ICDC international award winner, placing 4th in the world and sweeping all preliminary awards—proof of her consistent top-tier performance on the global stage. As Vice President of her 200+ member chapter, she not only excelled herself but also trained and mentored one of the largest teams in her state—all while graduating valedictorian, AP Scholar with Distinction, and a top-percentile Stanford researcher.

Now at the NYU Stern School of Business, Erika has turned her experience into coaching. In the past year alone, she's sent 25+ students to ICDC, with over 80% winning top awards. Her edge? Exclusive resources (no, not DECA+) powerful enough to take students from zero to 95+ scores in under two months.

What sets Erika apart isn't just her accolades, but her proven ability to transform students from average to exceptional—a skill even champions rarely replicate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this for?

High-school students preparing for DECA, FBLA, or NSDA competition who want structured, competitive preparation. Our current student results come from DECA coaching, and we are now accepting inquiries for the expanded next-season program.

Do you coach beginners?

Absolutely! We build fundamentals first — structure, voice, pacing — then layer advanced frameworks. Every student starts at their own level and follows a personalized improvement path.

Is coaching virtual?

Yes. All sessions run via Zoom with shared timers, prompts, and live feedback. This allows for efficient practice time, screen sharing for visual aids, and real-time recording for self-review.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the program intensity and schedule. We offer packages ranging from one-time competition prep to ongoing weekly sessions. Book a free intro call to discuss your specific needs and get detailed pricing options.

How long are sessions?

Standard sessions are 60 minutes, with extended 90-minute options available for advanced practice. Each session includes practice rounds, structured feedback, and targeted skill development tailored to the student's event format.

What events do you cover?

For the expanded next-season program, we accept DECA roleplay and written-event inquiries, FBLA presentation and competitive-event inquiries, and NSDA speech and debate inquiries. Include your organization and event name or code so we can confirm fit during your intro call.
