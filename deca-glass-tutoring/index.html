What DECA glass tutoring covers

Each tutoring plan is built around the event format, judging criteria, and practice habits that matter for your next competition.

Roleplay strategy

Structure cases quickly, connect performance indicators to realistic business decisions, use stronger terminology, and answer judge questions with confidence.

Written-event feedback

Turn an early idea into a clear competitive entry through stronger research, organization, executive summaries, visuals, and judge-friendly presentation choices.

Exam preparation

Identify the concepts costing you points, learn how DECA applies business vocabulary, and follow a focused practice plan instead of reviewing every topic equally.

Presentation coaching

Improve pacing, delivery, transitions, visual-aid use, and presence through timed repetitions and direct, specific feedback after every round.

A practice plan built around your event

Tutoring is designed to turn feedback into a repeatable process you can use under competition pressure.

Diagnose

Review your event, prior scores, recordings, and current approach to identify the highest-impact gaps.

Practice

Run targeted drills and realistic rounds that focus on one improvement goal at a time.

Review

Get precise feedback on content, delivery, timing, and strategic choices—not generic speaking advice.

Refine

Repeat the round with a clearer framework until the improvement holds when the prompt changes.
